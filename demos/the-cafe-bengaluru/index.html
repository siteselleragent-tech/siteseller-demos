Breakfast from 7:30 AM

A Bengaluru breakfast, on NIBM Road

The Cafe Bengaluru sits on the ground floor of Manish Plaza on NIBM Road. It opens at 7:30 AM on weekdays and stays open until 10 PM on weekends, which makes it one of the few places around Kondhwa Khurd where you can get a proper South Indian breakfast early and a filter coffee late.

Ghee podi dosa

The dish guests mention more than any other, named in 16 reviews, with the masala version close behind.

Filter coffee

Brewed the traditional way, and popular enough that the cafe also serves it as a softy and an ice cream.

Early opening

Doors open at 7:30 AM, so breakfast is possible before most of the city starts moving.

Value for money

Guests consistently describe the pricing as low for the portion size and quality on offer.
