Why the rating holds

A neighbourhood cafe done properly

The Coffee Coastal sits in Karishma Society in the Kothrud Industrial Area, close to Karve Road. It opens at noon and runs to midnight, which makes it a natural stop for an afternoon coffee or a late dinner. A 4.7 average across nearly twelve hundred reviews is unusual for a cafe of this size.

Homemade sauces

Reviewers repeatedly credit the in-house sauces and premium cheese for the taste difference.

Pasta-led menu

White sauce, baked peri peri and lasagna are the three most ordered dishes on the menu.

Jain-friendly options
