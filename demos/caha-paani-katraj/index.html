Welcome

Katraj's container cafe

Cahā Paani sits at Sr no 62/3 on the old Pune–Satara highway at Mangdewadi, on the Katraj side of the city. It is one of the few cafes in this stretch open from 7 AM, which makes it a genuine morning stop and not just an evening one.

Where it is

Old Pune–Satara Road, Mangdewadi, Katraj, Pune 411046. Easy to pull over for if you are driving in or out of the city.

Open early

Doors open at 7 AM and close at 11 PM, every day. Coffee before work is possible here.

Built from a container

The cafe itself is a converted cargo container, which is why the space feels small, warm and a bit unlike the usual Katraj cafe.
