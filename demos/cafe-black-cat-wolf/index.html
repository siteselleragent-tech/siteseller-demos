Our vibe

A cozy corner of Deccan Gymkhana

Lush greenery, cozy wooden seating and a relaxed cat theme — Black Cat & Wolf is the kind of place you don't rush. Meet Don, the in-house cat mascot, tuck into continental comfort food, and stay for the desserts. Two-wheeler parking available.
