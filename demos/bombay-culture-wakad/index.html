The cafe with flying noodles

Bombay Culture on Green Drive Road in Wakad runs an Asian and Continental menu out of a calm, well-kept room. Order the noodles wrapped around a fork, stay for the Thai curry rice, and take your time.

The dishes people name

Breaded from the dishes guests mention most often in the cafe's Google reviews, across Asian, Continental, bakery and breakfast.

🍜

Flying noodles

Saucy noodles with exotic vegetables, served in the presentation the cafe is known for. The most mentioned dish on the listing.

🍲

Tibetan thukpa

A noodle soup bowl that regulars keep coming back to, especially on a long evening.

🍛

Thai curry rice

A repeat favourite in reviews for the balance of the curry rather than the heat.

🍝

Peri peri pasta

Also on the menu as a creamy pasta. One of the more ordered continental plates.

🍳

English breakfast

Built on sourdough, with avocado toast and a hot latte alongside it.

🥤

Mocktails and coolers

Guava chilli cooler and mango iced tea get singled out in reviews.
