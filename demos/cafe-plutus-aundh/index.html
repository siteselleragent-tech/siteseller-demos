Why regulars keep coming back

Run by the person cooking your food

Cafe Plutus sits at Sai Heritage on the Aundh–Baner Link Road, a short walk from Medi Point Hospital. The thing guests mention most often is that the owner is also the chef, and that the buns and sauces are made on site rather than bought in.

In-house buns, never frozen

Guests repeatedly point out that the burger buns are freshly baked in house, and say it changes the texture of the whole burger.

Coffee that gets its own praise

Several reviews single out the coffee on its own, separate from the food, alongside the shakes and iced teas.

Built for working

Guests describe the AC section as a genuine work-from-cafe spot, with power plugs at each table and chilled infused water served in glass bottles.

Pet friendly

More than one review calls out the pet-friendly policy as a reason they chose this cafe over others nearby.
