The Qehhwa idea

Aesthetic ambience, work-friendly seating, honest food

Qehhwa sits just off NIBM Road with an indoor-outdoor layout, warm lighting and a menu that runs from breakfast pancakes to pasta, pizza and wings. Guests tend to settle in, so the seating and charging points are set up for it.

Specialty coffee

Cold coffee, hot chocolate and espresso drinks are the most mentioned items on the menu.

Global kitchen

Pastas, rosemary pizza, chicken wings and all-day breakfast plates.

Work-friendly seating

Charging points at multiple spots and a calm room through the afternoon.

Celebrations & groups

Indoor and outdoor seating for birthdays, catch-ups and small groups.
