The place

Ghar ka khana, cafe hours

Cafe Eshaara opens at 7 AM and runs to 10 PM, which covers breakfast, lunch, an afternoon coffee and an early dinner. The menu is built around homely Indian food: thali, poha, paratha, bhurji pav and bun maska, with cold coffee and Thai dishes alongside.

Breakfast from 7 AM

Cold coffee and cold brew

Cold coffee gets called out in reviews, with 12-hour steeped cold brew on the menu.

Thai and everyday plates
