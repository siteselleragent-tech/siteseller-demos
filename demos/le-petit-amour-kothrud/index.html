Why guests keep coming back

A little corner of France in Kothrud

Le Petit Amour sits at Shop 12, Damodar Villa, opposite Kothrud Bus Stand. Reviewers describe the room as a warm French bistro, small and cosy, with service that is polite without being intrusive. It is the kind of place people return to for a slow breakfast or an afternoon coffee and cake.

Patisserie that sells out

Desserts are the draw here. Guests regularly note that by evening the display is close to empty, so an early visit pays off.

Macarons and tiramisu

The two items named most often in reviews, alongside cheesecake and a tiramisu latte.

All-day brunch menu

Sandwiches, pizzas, burgers, fries and beverages are served right through the day, not just at breakfast.

Cosy, unhurried room

Described as small and comfortable, good for sitting with a book or catching up over a long coffee.
