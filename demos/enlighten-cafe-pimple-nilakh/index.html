Why people come back

A cafe built for slow mornings and long work sessions

Enlighten Cafe opened on New DP Road with a simple idea: great coffee, comfortable seating and a space quiet enough to actually get things done. The room is set up for both, with charging points, generous tables and a kitchen that runs all day.

Specialty coffee

Espresso, filter and slow brews pulled to order. Ask the barista what is on the grinder this week.

All-day kitchen

Breakfast plates, pastas, sandwiches and small plates served from open to close.

Coworking friendly

Wide tables, plug points at most seats and steady WiFi for the laptop crowd.

Meetings & groups

Quiet corners for two, larger tables for a team catch-up. Call ahead for groups of six or more.
