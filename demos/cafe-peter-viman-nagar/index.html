Where Viman Nagar hangs out.

Cafe Peter is a Pune cafe chain known for egg-less hand-made donuts, Korean fusion dishes, and real coffee. The Viman Nagar outlet, opposite Symbiosis International College, has become a student and young-professional favourite — buzzing through the day and well past midnight.
