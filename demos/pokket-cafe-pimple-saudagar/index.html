Why families book it

A cafe that hosts birthdays

Pokket Cafe sits at Shop B12 in Bhumi Allium, Prassana Colony, Pimple Saudagar. It runs a pure-veg menu across two levels, and a large share of its reviews are from parents who booked the space for a child's birthday. Guests repeatedly single out the manager, Poonam, for how smoothly those events run.
