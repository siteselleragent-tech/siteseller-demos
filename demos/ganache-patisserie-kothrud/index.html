Ganache Patisserie & Cafe

A small patisserie and cafe on Paud Road in Kothrud that has built its reputation on one thing above all: cheesecake. Alongside it, a short menu of gourmet sandwiches, pasta, waffles and coffee, at prices regulars describe as unusually fair.

Why people hunt it down

The cheesecake people come back for

Ganache is tucked into an older building near LIC Colony, opposite Domino's on Paud Road. It is small, and it fills up, especially with students from the colleges nearby. Reviewers call it a hidden gem and a treasure hunt to find, and almost all of them mention the same dessert.

Blueberry cheesecake

Named in 29 reviews, and repeatedly described as among the best in the city. Creamy, tangy and consistent.

Prices that stay fair

Guests describe the pricing as affordable for the quality, with no compromise on portion or ingredients.

Gourmet sandwiches

Chicken and roasted chicken sandwiches come up 20 and 14 times respectively, along with a high-protein range.

Pasta and pizza

Mac and cheese pasta and the pizza range are ordered as often as the desserts, with 26 pizza mentions.
