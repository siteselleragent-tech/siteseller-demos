Why people queue up

Chai, bun maska, and a table

A Kausar Baugh institution on Kondhwa Main Road. Regulars come for the Irani chai and the bun maska, and stay for the samosas and the unhurried morning rush. It is not a fancy room, and it does not try to be.

Bun maska, the house staple

The most-mentioned item on the menu, discussed in 88+ reviews, with a loyal following for the classic version.

What to order
